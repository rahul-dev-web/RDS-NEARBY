# RDS Nearby — Product Specification

## Product promise
**जो चाहिए, पहले अपने आस-पास देखो।**

Customer flow:
Need → Nearby shop → Availability/price → Contact/reserve → Buy → Reward → Repeat.

Merchant flow:
Join → Get QR → Bring customers → Earn Marketing Credits → Promote shop/offer → Get more customers.

## V1 scope
- Nearby business discovery and search
- Product/service discovery
- Availability, price and offers
- Call, WhatsApp, directions and reserve/contact
- Saved shops
- Local Points
- Customer referral
- Merchant business profiles, products/services, offers and requests
- Merchant referral QR and Marketing Credits
- Boost Shop and Promote Offer
- Request notifications and inbox
- Admin moderation, fraud, audit and analytics

## Explicit non-goals for V1
No delivery fleet, food/grocery delivery infrastructure, cash wallet withdrawals, merchant-to-merchant credit transfer, full ERP/inventory, complicated checkout, social feed, live chat, video calls, or multiple ad formats.

## Terminology
Customer reward: Local Points
Merchant reward: Marketing Credits
Free visibility: Organic Listing
Shop promotion: Boost Shop
Offer promotion: Promote Offer
Paid merchant plan: Local Business Growth
Customer action: Customer Request
Merchant request status: Accepting Requests

The word “Feature” is not a product term.

## Promotion
Boost Shop: 50 Marketing Credits / 24 hours.
Promote Offer: 100 Marketing Credits / 3 days.
Organic ranking remains meaningful; sponsored results are labelled.

## Referral
A qualified referral is not an install-only event. It requires signup, OTP verification, onboarding completion and meaningful activity within 48 hours. Valid activity includes search, shop/offer view, offer claim, request, save, call or WhatsApp. Referral state: PENDING → QUALIFIED, REJECTED or EXPIRED.

## Membership
Basic: ₹0.
Growth: ₹99/month, initially introduced only after pilot evidence. Initial pilot may run 30–60 days free.

## MVP boundary
The first usable MVP ends after customer discovery, merchant referral/credits, promotion and customer requests/notifications. Points redemption, advanced analytics, membership and Ask Nearby Shops are incremental layers.
