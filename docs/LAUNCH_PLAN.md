# Launch Plan

## Pilot

Start with one micro-market.

Target:
- 50–100 active shops
- 500–1,000 real users

Initial categories:
- Grocery
- Vegetable
- Salon
- Medical
- Stationery
- Fancy
- Mobile

## Merchant onboarding

Shop discovery
→ pitch
→ profile creation
→ QR activation
→ first offer
→ WhatsApp sharing

Keep merchant catalogue burden low.

## Customer acquisition

Primary channels:
- merchant QR
- WhatsApp status/share
- customer referrals
- public web shop pages
- counter QR posters/stickers

## Paid acquisition

Initial paid acquisition budget: ₹0.

First prove:
shops
→ QR
→ customers
→ referrals
→ repeat usage

Only then experiment with paid acquisition.

## Membership

Basic ₹0.

Growth ₹99/month.

Introduce paid membership only after the pilot demonstrates merchant value, ideally after a 30–60 day free pilot window.
